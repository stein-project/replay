# Replay: see how you died

Requires Stein Loader 0.1.28.

New mod. The game's death screen gives way to a replay of the last seconds of the fight.

- **Death in slow motion**: when you die, the screen turns red right away and the game keeps going in slow motion for a few seconds just as it was — first or third person, HUD and all —, showing you falling and your items scattering. Then the screen fades out and the replay comes in.
- **The replay is the respawn screen**: it loops, fading between rounds, until you respawn. In the top right corner are the **Respawn** and **Leave server** buttons.
- **Drone camera**: always looking the same way, never turning or switching sides, and never letting you leave the frame: it holds still while you are near the center and glides after you when you move away. It sees through blocks like spectator mode — falling into a hole shows from inside the hole.
- **Through your killer's eyes**: in the slow motion of the final blow, the camera goes into their view — the hand with the item, the swing, the particles, the shake when they get hit, the walking bob, the crosshair, their hearts and armor —, as if you were seeing their screen.
- **Killer card**: their head, name, held item and the armor they wore.
- **Hits and particles**: the red damage flash on whoever gets hit, the critical and sharpness sparks, the death smoke, potions and the other particles, each at its moment.
- **Sounds and the world as it was**: the fight's sounds play in the replay at their moment (deeper in slow motion), and the current world disappears: nothing left behind after your death shows up, nor the current sounds. Mobs, thrown potions, pearls and fishing lines are in too. The time of day and the rain are the fight's.
- **Skins**: the one the server sends and, on a server without authentication, the one of the original account with that nick, like in the Tab.
- **Saved replays**: every death is kept. On the Replay page of the Panel you see the list, watch them again (outside a server) and delete them. In saved replays: space pauses, left and right arrows go back and forward 5 s, up and down change the speed, click the bar to jump to a point, Esc goes back.
- **Always recording, no weight**: the last 30 seconds around you are kept all the time.
- Works with the new renderer (Stein GFX) and with the usual one.
- **Visual only**: it shows what your game already received from the server and sends nothing but the respawn request.
- On the Replay page of the Panel: on and off, length (5 to 30 s; 15 by default) and whether to save replays. Turned off (or in hardcore), the game's death screen is back.
