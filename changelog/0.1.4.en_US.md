# Replay 0.1.4: showing on death is now a choice

The replay keeps recording and saving your deaths, but it only shows on the respawn screen if you turn it on.

- **Show on death**: a new option, off by default. Whoever used the replay on death needs to turn it back on in the Panel.
- **Saved deaths**: nothing changes; they stay saved to watch again outside a server.
- **Servers**: a server can turn off the replay on the respawn screen while you play there.
- **Update notice**: the Loader's Panel shows when a new version of this mod is out.
